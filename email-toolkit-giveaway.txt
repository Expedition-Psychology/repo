WHAT THE TWO OF YOU WOULD BE WINNING

The complete Fear of Falling Toolkit, GBP 99 each and yours for life. It is a
step-by-step programme for retraining the fear, not a training plan - built on
the gold-standard psychological approach, and designed to slot into the
climbing you already do.

* 21 chapters and 24 video lessons, each with an interactive workbook built in.

* Fall-practice and exposure exercises in graded steps, so you never jump in
  too far, too fast.

* Strategies for staying calm on the wall - breathing, self-talk, visualisation
  - practised rather than just explained.

* A reflective log that tracks your fear over time and tells you when you are
  ready to move on, so you are not guessing.

* Goal setting and progress tracking, plus an online community of climbers
  working on the same thing.

Built by Dr Fin Haley, a chartered clinical psychologist and rock climbing
instructor, in partnership with Mammut and The Foundry Climbing Centre. It is
taught indoors; the skills carry over to outdoor sport climbing with the
appropriate risk assessment.
