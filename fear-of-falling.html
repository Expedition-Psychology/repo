What the Toolkit is, in 3 minutes

Sound Familiar?

Fear of falling holds back more climbers than weak fingers ever will.

You might notice it as any of these. None of it means you're “not brave enough.” Fear is a normal response — and like any response, it can be retrained.

Fear changes through experience, not avoidance. The Toolkit is built on the gold-standard psychological approach for overcoming fear.

Why It Usually Stalls

Just “taking more falls” rarely works

Most climbers run into the same four problems. The Toolkit is built around each one.

“I panic too soon and back off.”

You'll learn a range of practical strategies to stay calm on the wall, and practise them in graded steps — so you never jump in too far, too fast.

“I lose motivation after a few sessions.”

Concrete goals and progress tracking show you exactly how far you've come, and a community of climbers working on the same thing keeps you going.

“I can't fit it around my training.”

The plan slots into the climbing you already do, rather than the other way round.

“I don't know when I'm ready to push on.”

A reflective log tracks your fear over time and tells you when you're ready for the next exercise — so you're never guessing.
