What you actually get for £99

A structured, self-paced programme rather than another set of videos — here is what is in it, and how it fits around the climbing you already do.

You know what to work on. What is usually missing is a plan to follow, and something that tells you when you are ready for the next step.

That is the whole design brief for the Toolkit. It is self-paced, yours to keep, and built to run alongside your normal sessions rather than replace them.

How a week actually runs

Before the session

Ten minutes on a lesson and its workbook, then pick the exercise for today.

At the wall

Work the rung you are on. It is designed to sit inside a normal session, not add one.

Afterwards

Rate how it felt and note what happened. That rating is what paces the next step.
