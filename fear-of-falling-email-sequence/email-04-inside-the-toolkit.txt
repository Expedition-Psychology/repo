HOW A WEEK ACTUALLY RUNS

  * Before the session — Ten minutes on a lesson and its workbook, then
    pick the exercise for today.

  * At the wall — Work the rung you are on. It is designed to sit inside
    a normal session, not add one.

  * Afterwards — Rate how it felt and note what happened. That rating is
    what paces the next step.
