Setting the series up in Sender

Automation → trigger Subscriber Added to a Group on your confirmed list, then alternate Delay 1 month and Email steps, pasting each file in order.

The two after purchase emails are a separate automation: trigger on the Toolkit purchase group, then Delay 1 month → check-in, Delay 1 month → testimonial request. The second links to the testimonial form, which posts to Netlify Forms — answers appear under Forms → toolkit-testimonial in the Netlify dashboard.

Images are absolute URLs to this site, so they resolve in any client. The unsubscribe link uses Sender's {$unsubscribe_link} tag — leave it in place or Sender will refuse to send.
