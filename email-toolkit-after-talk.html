That is the gap the Fear of Falling Toolkit fills.

What it is

Twenty-one chapters and twenty-four videos, each with its workbook built in, so the thinking happens as you go rather than in a separate document you never open.

It runs in an app: your goals and your week on one screen, a quick way to log how each exercise actually felt, and a plan that uses those ratings to tell you when to hold at this level and when to move on. The progress stays visible, which matters most in the weeks when it does not feel like anything is changing.

There is also an online community of climbers working through the same thing, which helps more than people expect.
